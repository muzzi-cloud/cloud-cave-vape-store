Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Sample FAQs — customise these to match your real store and local requirements.

Is this a live store?

No. This is a front-end website demo. The sample cart does not process orders or payments, and the contact form does not send information to a business inbox.

Are these real products and prices?

No. Product names, visuals, descriptions, and prices are illustrative placeholders. Replace them with accurate, lawful information before publishing.

Can customers place an order here?

Not in this demo. A real checkout requires a secure backend, inventory management, an appropriate payment provider, order handling, and compliance checks.

How do I contact Cloud Cave?

Use the contact details supplied by the actual store owner. The contact form below is a visual demo and does not transmit messages.

Who can access vape products?

Only adults who meet the legal age in their jurisdiction, and only where sale and use are lawful. Local laws may restrict or prohibit these products.
